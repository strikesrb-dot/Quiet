## What v7 added (his asks, 2026-10-22)

He found the voice check unreliable ("on and off the voice model doesn't work") and asked for a blanket echo by sound
level, with muting of machine noise. Built:

- **Your voice | Sound level**, a picker on Home (under Silence | Conversation) and at the top of the session screen;
  one tap switches mid-session and the session keeps its numbers, its quiet timer and Talk freely's time left.
- **Sound level** (`LevelGate`, `src/pipeline.js`): any sound over the line opens the echo at once (after 30 ms over
  it, so a click can't) and holds it through dips (hold = echo delay + 150 ms); anything under is ignored. No phrases.
  All time over the line counts as his talk time; the session is marked Sound level (end sheet "Echo by", Past
  sessions, and `echoBy` in Claude's request, whose prompt now says that talk may include people near him). Samt
  learns nothing from it. Conversation's turn time and nudge work; "talking over someone" doesn't (no speaker check).
- **The line**: on the session screen, − and + beside the live pill move it (2 dB a tap, −66 to −16 dB); the level bar
  shows it and turns dark while over it. Shared by Light and Neural (`samt.shared.line`); until set it starts from the
  setup recordings.
- **Muted sounds** (Settings → Muted sounds, or Details in a session): listen 3 s while a machine runs and he's quiet,
  find its steady pitches (`findTones`, `src/dsp.js`), cut them with notch filters in the echo (`mic.js setNotches`)
  and in what the line measures. Up to 4 sounds; a sound spread over every pitch isn't muted ("raise the line").
- **Check it** (setup step 4) checks whichever is picked; with Sound level it can put the line between his typical
  level and the others' loudest (if 4 dB or more apart), or raise or lower it.
- **Phrases that pass removed everywhere** (his choice): the setup is 4 steps; no Settings row, page, Details count or
  Conversation offer. `PhraseBook` and the Teacher's 'take' mode are still in `pipeline.js`, unused; the Controller gets
  `book: null`. Backups are version 4 and carry an empty phrase list only so the v6 page can still restore them.
- **Session screen layout changed** to fit a phone: the picker at the top, then the time, then "SILENCE  Quiet for",
  then the pill (with − / + in Sound level) and the level bar. 795 px tall at 390 × 844 with a two-line verse.
- Fixed from an independent review: a mute listen that never got 3 s of audio could lock the app; the check showed NaN
  in Sound level; switching mid-session could inflate Longest quiet; a single click could open the echo; muting didn't
  always mark the check out of date; and an older bug (v5 on): after a Resume, the voice Controller's frame numbers
  weren't re-based, so Talk freely could block the echo for the length of the session so far.

## Lessons

- The test stream has only about 19.7 s of "me" talk clips; a round that must reach 20 s by itself needs them looped
  (`__T.clips` in `tests/sound-level.js` does).
- Sheets stack by z-index: regular sheets 41, Muted sounds 42/43 (it opens over Details), the shown card or sign 44,
  the action sheet 45/46, the text prompt 47/48. A sheet above 46 hides the action sheet that confirms things in it.
- `listener.reset()` (on Resume) restarts the Listener's frame count: both deciders need `resync(left)`, with Talk
  freely's time read before the reset.
- With voices as loud as his, no line splits them (the stream at full loudness: at best about 44% of others ignored,
  and the hard male voice never); with them 15 dB quieter, line −24 dB catches all of him and ignores all of them.
