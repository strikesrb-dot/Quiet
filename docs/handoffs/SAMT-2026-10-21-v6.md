## What v6 added (his ask: "wizardize the whole thing with other voices etc so I can do it all step by step")

- **Setup, step by step.** Home's first-open card is now "Set up Samt" → a page with a five-part bar and one step's
  card at a time: Your voice (the reading; the only step that can't be skipped) · Hear the echo · Other voices ·
  Check it · Phrases that pass (optional) · then a summary. Each step shows its result ("Done. Samt knows 43 seconds of
  your voice.") and, once done, Next as the main action. Settings → Your voice now starts with a **Step-by-step setup**
  row ("3 of 5", or "Done" after Finish); it reopens at the first unfinished step, or on the summary.
- **Check it (new).** Two rounds, no echo plays: you talk ~20 s, then someone else talks ~20 s (a Start tap between).
  It counts stretches of talk 1 s or longer: yours caught if the echo would have come on at any point; theirs ignored
  if it never would have, not even for a moment. Advice: both fine → "Samt can tell you apart."; took them for you →
  one tap makes Matching stricter and runs the check again (at Stricter already: add other voices, and in Light "Samt
  Neural tells voices apart better"); missed you → looser; both → teach more. Teaching more, or changing Matching, marks
  the result out of date. Nothing from the check is learned.
- State per version in localStorage `samt.<mode>.setup` (`skip`, `echoOk`, `check`, `done`); erasing the setup resets it.
- Code: `src/template.html` (`#vWizard`, its CSS, the `i-steps` icon, the Settings row, Home card copy), `src/ui.js`
  (the check, the wizard, hooks in `learn`, `endEchoTest`, erase and `goBack`; `SamtDebug.wiz()`, `SamtDebug.checkLog`),
  `tests/setup-flow.js` (the whole setup now goes through the wizard).

## Lessons

- The check is stricter than the test pass: for others it counts any moment of echo; the test pass counts the echo at
  a stretch's end.
- Neural verdicts need real time: `setup-flow.js` runs the check at fake-mic speed 1 for Neural (4 for Light). At 25
  the verdicts arrive after the stretch ends and look like misses.
- `features.js` in Neural can time out waiting for the model download through the proxy; rerun it before suspecting
  the code.
- In Playwright, `scrollHeight` can't show how tall a page's content is (the body has `min-height: 100dvh`); measure
  the bottom of `.win` plus its 32 px of padding.
