# Samt handoff, 2026-10-26 (v9: sessions up to 16 hours, recording a session, the echo switch while recording)

Samt (صمت, silence): a web app that plays the owner's own voice back ~200 ms late in his headphones (delayed auditory
feedback) so he talks less. Read this, then `SAMT-2026-10-22-v8.md` (the 60-second pause, Talk freely's limit; still
current except where this file says otherwise), `README.md` (its new "Recording" section) and `tests/README.md`.

## What v9 changed (his ask, 2026-10-26)

"Have it where i can set the timer for the sessions up to 16 hours. Also allow the session to be recorded. Allow a
remove echo toggle while recording so that i can record myself talking so much and listen to it later"

- **Up to 16 hours.** Presets 30 min, 1 hr, 2 hr, 8 hr, 16 hr (were 15 min to 4 hr); − / + move 5 minutes under an
  hour, 15 up to 4 hours, then an hour (5 min to 16 hr; the stepper is now "Length", shown as "2 hr 15 min"). "Until a
  time" counts up to 16 hours (a time further away is held to 16, with a toast). A time of an hour or more uses a
  smaller size so 16:00:00 fits a phone. Past sessions say "2 hr 15 min" instead of "135 min". The timer sheet says to
  keep Samt open with the screen on and to plug in for a long session.
- **Record** sits in the session row: Card · Sign · Record (the main button under them on a phone; one line on a wide
  screen). It records the mic only, never the echo: MediaRecorder on the mic's own stream (iPhone: AAC, .m4a, asked
  for at 32 kbps, about 15 MB an hour), else 16 kHz WAV from the chunks the listener gets. A tap stops it; a session
  can have several; a test none. While it runs the button is red with its time. Parts go to their own IndexedDB
  database (`samt-rec`) every 10 s; the session is listed with its recording at once.
- **The echo switch.** While a recording runs, Talk freely's button reads "Turn the echo off" / "Turn the echo back on",
  with no 20-second or hourly limit; the pill says "Echo off". It's a Talk freely with no end (24 h) that isn't counted
  as a tap, so no echo and no nudges, and his talk still counts. Stopping the recording turns the echo back on.
- **Afterwards**, the session's sheet (at the end, and from Past sessions, marked "Recorded") lists "Recordings": each
  with its time, size and length, and Play, Save (`samt-YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM.m4a`) and Delete. Deleting a session or clearing
  the past sessions deletes its recordings. Backup files carry the list of recordings, not the audio (the sheet and the
  backup hint say so).

## His decisions this chat (keep them)

- A Record button in the session (Card · Sign · Record), to start and stop recording any time (2026-10-26).
- Timer presets 30 min, 1, 2, 8 and 16 hr (2026-10-26).
- The plan he accepted with those two answers: sessions up to 16 hours (past 4 hours, − and + move an hour; "Until a
  time" up to 16 hours away; the phone stays unlocked with Samt open, on a charger); recording is the mic only, never
  the echo, about 15 MB an hour, kept on the phone, and in the session's sheet under Progress to play, save to Files
  or delete; while recording, Talk freely becomes an Echo off / Echo on switch with no limits, and the echo comes back
  when the recording stops.

## Calls made without asking him (tell him; each is easy to change)

- A held pause doesn't stop a recording (the mic is still on); a call, Siri or the screen locking does, and what it got
  is kept.
- A recording under a second isn't kept ("Too short to keep.").
- Recording needs 25 MB free to start.
- A session with a recording is kept however short (others still need 30 seconds).

- Chromium's `decodeAudioData` empties the ArrayBuffer it's given: read `byteLength` first.
