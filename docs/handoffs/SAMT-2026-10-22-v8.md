## What v8 changed (his asks after trying v7, 2026-10-22)

- **No double-tap zoom** ("Double tap ignore": two quick taps on − / +, Talk freely or a stepper zoomed the page on his
  iPhone): every element is `touch-action: manipulation`; pinch zoom still works; the hold button stays `none`.
- **No text highlighting** ("text highlight pops up too loosely"): `user-select: none` and no press-and-hold callout on
  the page; inputs and textareas (the note, a card, the sign's words) keep selection.
- **The main button: a 2-second hold pauses the session for up to 60 s** (was: ends it). Paused: the echo stops, the
  mic keeps running but its sound is set aside (no counting; iPhone Safari won't reliably restart a mic without a tap,
  so it isn't let go), the clock stands still, and the pill counts down "Echo back in 0:45"; at 0 the echo is back by
  itself. The screen shows Resume (the dark capsule, ends the pause early), Card, Sign and End session (one tap). A
  call or Siri also pauses it, with no time limit, until Resume (the clock stands still there too now). A test still
  stops with a tap.
- **Talk freely: 20 s a tap** (was 10), up to 60 s at once. **Any 3 taps within 10 minutes turn it off for an hour**
  (the third tap still counts). Kept on the device for Light and Neural alike (`samt.shared.freeTaps`, `freeLock`), so
  ending a session doesn't reset it. While it runs, the pill counts down "Echo back in 0:14" and the button says "Add
  20 s · 1 left"; while it's off, "Talk freely in 59:12".
- **"Echo back in"** (his ask: "Add the time to hear echo again"): on the pill for both the pause and Talk freely.

## Lessons

- With the test mic, a session's length is audio time, and a Resume restarts the Listener's frames: `session.fakeSec`
  carries the audio time across a pause so the end sheet (sessions of 30 s or more) still opens in the tests.
- Playwright's WebKit on Linux has no iOS double-tap zoom, so the tests check the computed `touch-action` instead
  (`features.js`: every element `manipulation`, Stop `none`).
