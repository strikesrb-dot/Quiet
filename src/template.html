Muted sounds

None yet.

That's the most Samt keeps. Unmute one to add another.

While the sound is going and you stay quiet, tap Mute a sound. Samt listens for 3 seconds, finds the sound's strongest pitches and cuts them from the echo and from what the line measures. It works for steady hums and whines, like a generator, a fan or an engine's whine. A roar spread over every pitch, like wind, can't be cut without cutting your voice too; raise the line above it instead.
