Conversation

Talk before the echo
15 s

For Conversation sessions. Each turn you can talk this long before the echo starts. A new turn begins when someone else speaks, or when you stay quiet for a second and a half.

No interrupting: talking over someone starts the echo at once. Nudge: a soft two-note tone in your headphones about 2½ seconds before the echo.

Teach Samt

Voice modelLoading

Your voice: read a short passage out loud, about 45 seconds, with the headphones and in the room you'll use. Add more any time, on other days and in other moods; Samt also keeps learning while you use it.

Other voices: let 30 seconds of other people talk while you stay quiet, such as the TV, a video, a call on speaker or someone in the room.

Phrases that pass

Say each phrase 3 times. When Samt hears one of them from you, it lets it through with no echo. Replies shorter than half a second already pass.

Matching phrases
