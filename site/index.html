Samt

A quiet-time aid

Samt (silence) plays your own voice back to you in your headphones a fraction of a second late, which makes talking very hard. It can do this for your voice alone, or for any sound over a loudness line you set.

Choose a version

Samt LightOpens instantly Samt NeuralMost accurate

Light is one small page. It tells you apart from women, children and the TV well, but is less sure against other men.

Neural adds an on-device voice-ID model, about 17 MB on first open, and is much better at telling you apart from other men. It needs iPadOS 16.4 or newer.

Each version keeps its own setup. Wear headphones, keep the page open in front while it runs, and don't use it at the ramp or jetbridge, or while driving.
